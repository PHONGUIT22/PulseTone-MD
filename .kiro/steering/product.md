# Steering Directive: Product Vision & User Personas

## Target Personas

### 1. Eleanor Vance (Age 78) - Patient
- **Condition:** Advanced Parkinson's disease with moderate intention tremors, early-stage macular degeneration.
- **Pain Point:** Cannot reliably navigate modern smartphone capacitive touchscreens, capacitive glass gestures, or two-factor SMS push notifications.
- **Resurrection Bridge:** Eleanor has 50+ years of landline phone muscle memory. PulseTone-95 gives her physical/tactile 1995 DTMF dual-tone telephone buttons (`1#`, `2#`, `3#`, `0#`, `9#`) that produce audible, reassuring acoustic feedback while invisibly orchestrating advanced agentic healthcare workflows behind the scenes.

### 2. Sarah Connor (Age 48) - Remote Caregiver & Daughter
- **Role:** Working professional living 45 minutes away; oversees Eleanor's chronic care regimen and medication adherence.
- **Pain Point:** Suffers from mobile app notification fatigue and delayed alert delivery.
- **Resurrection Bridge:** Receives instant, high-priority 1995 Motorola POCSAG Alphanumeric Pager alerts with the signature 2400 Hz acoustic chirp for inventory drop ($\le 3$ pills), Beers Criteria drug interactions, or budget-exceeding refills. Monitored via the AG Grid Clinical-Financial Ledger.

## Product Guardrails
1. **Zero Frustration:** Every DTMF keystroke must generate instant acoustic and visual confirmation.
2. **Fail-Safe Autonomy:** Autonomous commerce is capped at $50.00/month. Any charge exceeding the threshold immediately alerts the caregiver via POCSAG telemetry.
3. **Clinical Grounding:** All AI recommendations must conform to the 2023 AGS Beers Criteria.
